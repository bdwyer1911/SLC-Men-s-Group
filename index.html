Men Need Community.

Honest conversations. Meaningful friendships. Shared adventures.

SLC Men's Group is a place for personal growth, authentic connection, and a community of men who show up for one another.

Our Mission

To create a safe space for men to interact in a supportive community, have honest and powerful conversations, and experience growth in meaningful ways. Men thrive when they have a place to be known, supported, and encouraged by other men.

Why We Exist

  • Many men experience pressure, anxiety, and isolation.
  • Real friendship and honest conversation can be hard to find.
  • We want to create healthy community and meaningful connection.

What We Do

  • Share openly about life and personal growth.
  • Support each other without judgment or competition.
  • Build lasting friendships grounded in honesty.

What To Expect

  • Informal and welcoming discussions.
  • No pressure to share.
  • Listening is participation too.
  • A safe place to be vulnerable and authentic.

More Than Meetings

Community grows through shared experiences. Outside our discussions, members often connect through hiking, backpacking, biking, service opportunities, and adventures throughout Utah.

Meetings

When: Every other Monday evening, typically lasting ~2 hours

Where: Usually at a local library, with occasional gatherings at other locations around the Salt Lake area.

Atmosphere: Open, relaxed, respectful, and supportive. The meetings are informal, round table discussions.

Participation: You are never required to share. Many people start by simply listening.

Community: Members also stay connected through a private Discord community between meetings.
